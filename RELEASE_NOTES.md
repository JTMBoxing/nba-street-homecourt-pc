# NBA Street Homecourt – PC Port (Beta 0.1)

A native Windows port of NBA Street Homecourt (Xbox 360). **This download does NOT include the game:** you need your own NBA Street Homecourt (USA) `.iso`. Run `Install.exe` and pick your `.iso`.

## New players (18)
Each with his face, hair, tattoos, body, gear and his own jump shot.
- **Knicks:** Jalen Brunson, Karl-Anthony Towns, OG Anunoby, Mikal Bridges, Josh Hart, Jordan Clarkson
- **76ers:** Joel Embiid, Tyrese Maxey, VJ Edgecombe, LeBron James, Jaylen Brown
- **Celtics:** Jayson Tatum, Paul George, Baylor Scheierman
- **Timberwolves:** Anthony Edwards, LaMelo Ball, Jaden McDaniels, Ayo Dosunmu

Removed to make room: Eddy Curry, Steve Francis, Wally Szczerbiak, Samuel Dalembert.
Updated uniforms with names and numbers for the new players.

## Courts
- **Venice** is the only court with new changes in this beta (new colours and surfaces).

## Gameplay
- New controller layout (or the original buttons — your choice at install)
- Random dunks with turbo, random layups without
- Signature dribble moves on the right stick
- Drive camera that follows the ball (Back / F2 for the camera menu)
- Inbounds from behind the baseline, no full-court press
- Add your own music to the soundtrack

## Updates
Unzip a newer release over your folder and run `Install.exe` again. Only changed files are written; your saves are kept.

## Bug reports
Send `nbash_log.txt` (and `install_log.txt` for install problems) and say what you were doing.

## Known limits
HD court textures aren't in this beta yet. Windows 10/11 64-bit. Tested on one PC so far.
